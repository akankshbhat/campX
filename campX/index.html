Frequently asked questions

How CampX works, who can join, and what happens during an exchange.

What is CampX?

CampX is a campus-sharing app for B.M.S. College of Engineering students. Instead of buying something you need for a week, you can borrow it, rent it, buy it second-hand or sell what you no longer use to another student on campus.

Who can use CampX?

Only BMSCE students with an official @bmsce.ac.in email address can register and log in. Personal addresses from Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or any other domain are not accepted, so every listing and request comes from a verified member of the college community.

How do I create an account?

Open Log in, choose Create account, and enter your full name, @bmsce.ac.in email, student ID, college and a password of at least 8 characters. Your account is ready immediately — there is no confirmation email, OTP or verification link to wait for.

Why is my email address rejected?

CampX only accepts addresses that end exactly in @bmsce.ac.in. Look-alike domains such as notbmsce.ac.in or bmsce.ac.in.example.com are refused. If you see the access-restricted message, sign in with your official college address.

How do I log in?

Click Log in, enter your @bmsce.ac.in email and password, then press Log in or hit Enter. Email matching ignores capitals, so Student@BMSCE.ac.in and student@bmsce.ac.in are the same account. Your session lasts about a week; after that, log in again.

How do I list an item?

Click List an item, add a name and description, pick a category and condition, choose free, rent or sale, set a price and optional refundable deposit, add a pickup spot and up to four photos (JPG, PNG or WebP under 3 MB each), then press Publish listing. You can remove your own listing from its detail view.

How do requests and dates work?

Open a listing, choose your pickup and return dates, add an optional message and send the request. The owner accepts or declines it. Rental fees are calculated per day, and dates already booked by an accepted request cannot be double-booked.

How does the handover and return work?

Meet on campus and both of you press Confirm handover so the exchange is recorded. When the item goes back, both sides press Confirm return and the request is marked completed. Overdue rentals are flagged in My requests.

What about payments and deposits?

CampX does not process payments. Prices, rental fees and deposits are shown in the app, but money is settled directly between students when you meet. A rental deposit is returned by the owner once the item comes back in the same condition.

What happens if something goes wrong?

During an active exchange the owner can report damage, and either side can open a dispute with a short written reason. The request is then marked In dispute for review, and both students keep the full history in My requests.

How is the impact on My requests counted?

The numbers come only from exchanges that both sides marked completed. CampX does not estimate environmental savings — only real, finished activity on your account is counted.

Does CampX cost anything?

Browsing, listing and requesting are free. A listing owner may charge a rental fee or a sale price, and may ask for a refundable deposit on rentals, which is agreed between the two students.

About the platform

What CampX is

A sharing marketplace for BMSCE students. Every listing is a piece of campus equipment, a textbook, a lab coat or something similar that can change hands between students instead of being bought new.

What it solves

Most items a student needs are used for a few days or one semester. Buying them new is expensive and leaves them unused afterwards. CampX connects the student who needs an item with the student who already owns one, with dates and a pickup spot agreed before the handover.

Key features

  • Browse and search by item name, by free, rent or sale, and by category, sorted by newest or price.
  • List an item with a description, condition, category, price or per-day fee, an optional refundable deposit, a pickup spot and up to four photos.
  • Send a request with pickup and return dates; the fee is calculated from the days you need, and already booked dates cannot be double-booked.
  • Confirm handover and return from both sides, so every exchange has a record, with damage reports and disputes if something goes wrong.
  • Track your impact in My requests, counted only from exchanges both students marked completed.
  • BMSCE-only access with an official @bmsce.ac.in address, so every listing and request comes from a member of the college.

Who it is for

Students who need something briefly, students with spare things at home, and anyone who would rather buy a used textbook than a new one. There are no fees for browsing, listing or requesting — money for a rental, a sale or a deposit is settled directly between the two students when they meet on campus.

Mission, purpose and place

Our mission

Make sharing the default on campus: give every student a simple, trustworthy way to find what they need and pass on what they do not, so fewer things are bought once and forgotten.

Why this platform?

Campus message groups and notice boards lose track of who has what, who booked which dates and whether an item came back. CampX keeps listings, dates, requests and confirmations in one place, restricted to verified college addresses, which is the part an informal group chat cannot do.

Our institution

CampX is a student project from B.M.S. College of Engineering (BMSCE), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The platform is designed around campus life there, and access is limited to the college's own email domain.
